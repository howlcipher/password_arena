EVALUATION_FINDING: CALIBRATION LIMITATION

Benchmark 002 exposed an important limitation in relying solely on survival metrics: A bounded strategy attacker may fail to guess a weak synthetic target.

For instance, the weakest Qwen defender generated this target:

  • Length: 2
  • Entropy: 6.58 bits
  • Family: cryptographic-random

It survived 500 guesses against the Qwen3 4B attacker. A length=2 string is trivial to brute-force, but a bounded agent targeting complex password patterns may entirely omit simple exhaustive searches from its strategy.

Therefore Password Arena evaluates: solve/survival + entropy + target structure + strategy coverage + tokens + latency
